A Dual Crisis, Solved.

Bridging the civic knowledge gap around One Nation One Election while deploying state-of-the-art AI to fight political deepfakes.

ONOE Digital Twin

Interactive Indian map visualizing the High-Level Committee's recommendations. Clearly track state tenure curtailments (Red) and extensions (Green) aligned with the 2029 election cycle.

Anti-Misinformation Suite

Our Forensic Lab detects AI-generated content and deepfakes using DeBERTa models. View forensic heatmaps, AI confidence scores, and blockchain-logged immutable verdicts on viral claims.

ECI Situation Room

Your single source of truth. Get live, verified updates directly from the Election Commission. Access original PDF circulars, legal guidelines, and simplified summaries of complex electoral procedures.

360° Party View Hub

Access a neutral, side-by-side comparison of party manifestos and policies. A dedicated Civic AI Assistant explains voting rules and constitutional changes in your regional language.

Verified & Private Access

Integrated securely with DigiLocker and Aadhaar APIs. Zero-Knowledge Proofs ensure your identity stays private while completely eliminating bots and coordinated manipulation on the platform.

Scan & Verify

Designed to integrate with voter slip QR codes for instant, last-mile verified awareness on election day.
